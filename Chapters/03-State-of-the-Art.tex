\chapter{State of the Art}
\label{cha:state-of-the-art}

This chapter examines existing research on telemetry correlation,
security-relevant data, agent-based diagnosis and controlled remediation.
These topics support the four research questions introduced in
Section~\ref{sec:introduction:research-questions}. The comparison focuses
on what the approaches require, how they have been evaluated and what
their results support in relation to the focused Norlys proof of concept
\parencite[Scope]{norlys_operations_agents}.

The review is intended to inform three parts of the project. First, it
provides evidence for design choices, such as how agents gather and share
diagnostic information. Second, it helps identify evaluation methods for
assessing diagnoses and remediation outcomes. Third, it establishes which
questions remain unresolved within the reviewed literature. The
experiments in this thesis must then assess the implemented system in
the selected environment.

The review uses IEEE Xplore and OpenAlex as its main search
sources. Appendix~\ref{app:systematic-literature-review} documents the
protocol and provides the location for the detailed review records and
study assessments. The main chapter presents the findings needed to
understand the project's contribution, including conflicting evidence
and limitations that affect its interpretation.
